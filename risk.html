02.General Trading Risks

Trading financial instruments involves a variety of risks. Below are the most significant categories of risk you should be aware of before engaging in any trading activity.

📉 Market Risk

Prices can move rapidly against your position, resulting in losses that may exceed your expectations.

⚡ Leverage Risk

Leverage amplifies both profits and losses. Small market movements can lead to significant losses.

💧 Liquidity Risk

Markets may lack sufficient liquidity, making it difficult to open or close positions at desired prices.

🌐 Volatility Risk

Market volatility may cause rapid price swings, resulting in stop-loss slippage or premature liquidations.

🌙 Gap Risk

Markets can gap over weekends or news events, causing positions to open at prices significantly different from the last close.

📰 News Risk

Economic news, geopolitical events, and central bank decisions can cause extreme volatility.

💻 Technical Risk

Platform outages, connectivity issues, or software errors may prevent you from managing positions.

💱 Currency Risk

Fluctuations in exchange rates may affect the value of your account and payouts.

03.Leverage & Margin

Leverage allows you to control a large position with a relatively small amount of capital. While leverage can amplify profits, it can equally amplify losses. A small adverse price movement can result in the loss of your entire account balance.

You must understand that:

  • Leverage magnifies both profits and losses proportionally.
  • Using high leverage significantly increases the risk of rapid account depletion.
  • You may be required to deposit additional funds (margin calls) to maintain open positions.
  • Positions may be closed automatically (liquidated) if margin requirements are not met.
  • Trading on margin is not suitable for all investors.

Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose. Leverage should be used responsibly and only after a thorough understanding of its mechanics.
